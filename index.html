Emerald property sales system

Emerald Star Company Limited · Tema, Ghana

Emerald is the internal system that Emerald Star Company Limited uses to manage the sale of land: enquiries from prospective buyers, plot allocation on site plans, sale terms and instalment plans, payments, and the Customer Information Form and Land Purchase Agreement.

The system is used only by authorised Emerald staff. It is not a public service and customers do not sign in to it.

Google Drive
Emerald stores its documents (site plans, Ghana Card copies, contracts, signed copies and payment proofs) privately in the company's own Google Drive. It asks Google only for permission to see the files and folders it creates itself, and the email address of the company account that connects it. See the privacy policy.
