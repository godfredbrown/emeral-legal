Privacy policy

Emerald property sales system · Last updated 11 October 2026

This policy explains how Emerald Star Company Limited ("Emerald", "we") handles personal data in its internal property sales system ("the system"). We handle personal data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Who is responsible

Emerald Star Company Limited, Tema Community 25, Ghana. Contact: emeraldstar.gh@gmail.com, 055 349 7597 / 020 906 3077.

What we collect

  • Prospective buyers and customers: name, phone numbers, email, residential address, occupation, nationality, Ghana Card number and copies of the Ghana Card, next of kin, the plots of interest or purchased, agreed prices, payment plans and payments made.
  • Staff who use the system: name, work email, phone, role, and a record of their actions in the system (an audit log).

Why we use it

To respond to enquiries, allocate and sell land, prepare and keep sale agreements, record and account for payments, meet our legal and tax obligations, and protect against fraud and mistakes.

Where it is kept

The system runs on a computer at Emerald's office. Documents and images are stored privately in Emerald's own Google Drive account, in a folder named "Emerald Star Company Limited". Files are never shared with a public link; staff open them only through the system, which checks their role first, and every view of a Ghana Card copy and every document download is recorded.

Google user data

When an Emerald administrator connects the system to the company's Google account, the system asks Google for two things only:

  • Google Drive, limited to files the system creates (the "drive.file" permission). The system uses this to create its own folders and to save and read back the files it stores there. It cannot see, change or delete any other file in that Google Drive.
  • The email address of the connected Google account, to show which account is connected.

Data obtained through Google is used only to provide these storage features to Emerald. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone else, and not used to train any AI or machine-learning model. Emerald's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The connection can be removed at any time from the system's Settings page or from the Google account's security settings.

Who can see it

Only authorised Emerald staff, according to their role: for example, a Site Manager sees only the customers assigned to them. We do not sell personal data. We share it only where the law requires, with our legal advisers, or with service providers that host or store it for us (such as Google for file storage).

How long we keep it

For as long as needed for the sale and for the period required by law for property, contract and financial records. Records of completed sales, payments and signed agreements are kept permanently as the company's legal record.

Security

Access needs a personal account and password; administrators also use a one-time code from an authenticator app. Sessions end after 30 minutes of inactivity. Connections are encrypted, and key business records cannot be altered after the event.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, or to raise a concern about how it is used. Contact us using the details above. You may also contact the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
