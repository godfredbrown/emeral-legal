Terms of use

Emerald property sales system · Last updated 11 October 2026

These terms apply to everyone who uses the Emerald property sales system ("the system") operated by Emerald Star Company Limited ("Emerald").

1. Who may use it

The system is for authorised Emerald staff only. Accounts are created by Emerald's Super Admin and are personal: do not share your password or authenticator code, and tell the Super Admin straight away if you think your account has been misused.

2. Proper use

  • Use the system only for Emerald's business, and only for the customers and tasks your role allows.
  • Enter information accurately and promptly. Never record a payment, document or plot status you know to be wrong.
  • Keep customers' personal data, including Ghana Card copies, confidential. Do not copy it out of the system except for Emerald's business.
  • Do not try to get around the system's permissions or security.

3. Records

Actions in the system are recorded with your name and the time. Payments, approved sale terms and signed documents are kept as Emerald's business records and cannot be deleted; mistakes are corrected by a new entry (for example, a reversal).

4. Documents

Contracts produced by the system use the wording approved by Emerald's lawyers. A document is binding only once signed by the parties; the system never signs anything on anyone's behalf.

5. Google Drive

The system stores files in Emerald's own Google Drive with access limited to the files it creates. Use of Google services is also subject to Google's terms.

6. Availability

Emerald aims to keep the system available but does not guarantee uninterrupted service, for example during power cuts or maintenance.

7. Suspension

Emerald may suspend or remove an account at any time, for example when someone leaves the company or breaks these terms.

8. Privacy

How personal data is handled is explained in the privacy policy.
